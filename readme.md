## 📕 Content
This mod adds DayZ-style melee combat to ArmA.  
- Melee with fists, rifles, pistols and dedicated melee weapons  
↳ Main & Alternate Attack  
↳ Blocking  
↳ Kicking  
↳ Pushing  
↳ Dodging  
↳ Stunning/Disarming  
↳ Takedowns (Fists/Knives)  
- AI Melee when disarmed or using the "AI Melee" Zeus module.
- Ability to cut down trees/bushes.
- Weapons  
↳ M9 Bayonet  
↳ OKC-3S Bayonet  
↳ AK-74 Bayonet  
↳ MPL-50 Entrenching Tool  
↳ Kabar  
↳ SOG Throwing Knife  
↳ SOCP Dagger  
↳ Rubber Training Knife  
↳ Kitchen Knife  
↳ Gavel  
↳ Splitting Axe  
↳ Fire Axe  
↳ Sledgehammer  
↳ Halligan Bar  
↳ Cavalry Saber  

## 🔥 Compatibility
✔️ Works with [ACE](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=463939057) medical.  
✔️ If loaded with [Breach](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3283645995), you will be able to breach doors with melee.  
✔️ Bayonets are compatible with [RHS](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=843770737) M16/M4 and AK-74.  
✔️ Compatible with [SOGPF](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1227700/Arma_3_Creator_DLC_SOG_Prairie_Fire/) bayonets & melee weapons.  
✔️ Compatible with [3CB BAF Weapons](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=893339590) bayonet.  
✔️ [Phenosi's IMS Compat](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3286427890) makes melee weapons use [IMS](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2291129343) instead.  
❌ Likely not compatible with other mods that have similar features.  
❌ Not compatible with older versions of this mod.  

⚠️ The [Cavalry Saber](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cavalry-saber-ww1-sabre-sword-94ba67d2f393457e8ee9d2d1fb2d7178) is subject to its own license.
